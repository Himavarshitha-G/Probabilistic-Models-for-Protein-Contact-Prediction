# Development of Probabilistic Models for Predicting Long-Range Contacts in Protein Structures

## Overview

This project focuses on predicting long-range residue contacts in protein structures using probabilistic and statistical modeling approaches.

The study analyzes residue interactions, structural distance relationships, and amino acid pair distributions to understand protein folding and structural organization.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze residue-residue interactions in protein structures
* Generate distance and contact matrices
* Study amino acid pair distributions
* Apply probabilistic and statistical modeling techniques
* Evaluate structural interaction patterns in proteins

---

## Methodology

1. Selection of protein structure datasets
2. Generation of residue distance matrices
3. Contact prediction analysis
4. Statistical and probabilistic modeling
5. Validation and visualization of interaction patterns
